Frequently Asked Questions

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                How is the ATS Score calculated?
                                expand_more
                            

                            

                                The ATS Score is evaluated using the Llama 3.3 model. It performs semantic mapping between the resume's parsed experiences/skills and the job description requirements. It grades formatting, technical keywords overlap, and work experience impact to return a value from 0 to 100.
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                Can I use other LLMs?
                                expand_more
                            

                            

                                Currently, CareerNexus uses Groq's high-speed endpoint (Llama-3.3-70b-versatile) which provides near-instant completions ideal for real-time analysis. Support for custom endpoints is coming in future updates.
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                How does the job description filtering work?
                                expand_more
                            

                            

                                When a search campaign is launched, the scraper filters out closed postings. The ATS scoring agent also evaluates listings for expressions like 'expired' or 'closed' and filters them, providing you with direct apply links to only active jobs.

Frequently asked questions

        
        

            
            

                

                    How does CareerNexus calculate my ATS score?
                    expand_less
                

                

                    
CareerNexus employs an advanced dual-pass evaluation engine powered by Llama 3.3 70B intelligence.

                    
Pass 1 (Parsability Audit): Evaluates document structural extractability, section header compliance, font hierarchy, and date formatting against real-world ATS software standards (such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and BambooHR).

                    
Pass 2 (Semantic Relevance): Compares your experience bullets against target job descriptions, grading technical keyword overlap, action verb density, and quantified achievement ratios to generate an overall 0-100% compatibility rating.

                

            


            
            

                

                    Is my uploaded resume kept private and secure?
                    expand_less
                

                

                    
Yes. Absolute candidate privacy and data security are core design principles of CareerNexus.

                    
All uploaded PDF or DOCX files are parsed in isolated session contexts and stored strictly within your personal, encrypted local SQLite database. We never sell candidate data to 3rd party brokers, share profiles with recruiters without explicit authorization, or retain documents for AI model training.

                

            


            
            

                

                    What is considered a passing ATS score?
                    expand_less
                

                

                    
A overall match score of 75% or higher is considered optimal for passing automated ATS filters and securing recruiter callbacks.

                    
Scores in this range demonstrate strong structural readability and high technical keyword alignment. If your profile scores below 75%, our ATS Scorer tab highlights missing hard/soft skills and provides color-coded bullet rewrites to boost your rating into the interview tier.

                

            


            
            

                

                    How do custom Groq API keys work?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
CareerNexus includes default built-in server-side AI credits for immediate out-of-the-box testing.

                    
However, if you input your own free Groq API key in the Settings tab, all LLM reasoning requests (ATS grading, AI career coaching, company research, and outreach letter writing) route directly through your personal developer quota, unlocking maximum Llama-3.3 inference speed and zero shared rate limits.

                

            


            
            

                

                    Can CareerNexus parse multi-page PDF resumes?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
Yes. CareerNexus handles single-page and multi-page PDF and DOCX files with ease.

                    
For maximum parsing accuracy across commercial recruitment tools, we recommend utilizing standard single-column layouts with clear text hierarchies rather than complex multi-layered vector graphics or embedded image tables.

                

            


            
            

                

                    How does the Career AI Coach assist my job search?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
The Career AI Coach is an interactive chat mentor equipped with full semantic memory of your loaded resume and active target job descriptions.

                    
You can use the mentor to conduct mock technical interviews, practice behavioral STAR-format responses, draft custom elevator pitches, or debug specific rejection reasons.

                

            


            
            

                

                    What details are revealed by the Company Intelligence feature?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
The Company Intelligence agent performs deep-dive background research across Wikipedia and public company APIs to build detailed candidate dossiers.

                    
It uncovers workforce headcount estimates, revenue brackets, matching vs. missing tech stack components, corporate culture tags, main market competitors, funding stages, and customized interview cheat sheets.

                

            


            
            

                

                    How do Multi-Agent Campaigns automate applications?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
Agent Campaigns orchestrate specialized AI agents to automate your job application pipeline.

                    
Upon entering a search query (e.g. "Full Stack Engineer in India"), scraping agents pull live job postings, filter out expired roles, compute ATS compatibility scores, generate tailored Cover Letters, draft recruiter outreach emails, and log every application into your SQLite database.

                

            


            
            

                

                    Can I synchronize my job search with Notion?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
Yes! CareerNexus features native integration with Notion databases.

                    
By providing your Notion Integration Token and Database ID in the Settings tab, any job discovered during automated campaigns is automatically added as a new row in your personal Notion workspace complete with company name, job URL, fit score, and application date.

                

            


            
            

                

                    How is CareerNexus different from standard resume checkers?
                    expand_more
                

                

                    
Unlike simple spellcheckers or basic keyword counters, CareerNexus is a unified multi-agent career copilot.

                    
It brings together ATS scoring, real-time AI interview coaching, corporate intelligence research, automated job scraping, custom outreach writing, and Notion CRM synchronization in one single platform.
